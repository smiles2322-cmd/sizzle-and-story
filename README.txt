SIZZLE & STORY  (version 3: Blind & Low Vision mode)
Cook the story. Make the meal.

HOW TO OPEN IT
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Double-click index.html. It opens in your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox).
   That's it. No internet or account needed.

ON A PHONE
- To install it as an app on a phone, the folder has to be put on a website (for example
  Netlify Drop, GitHub Pages, or Lovable hosting). Then open the link on the phone and choose
  "Add to Home Screen". It works offline after the first visit.
- Play online: https://smiles2322-cmd.github.io/sizzle-and-story/

WHAT'S NEW IN VERSION 3
- Blind & Low Vision mode (off by default). Turn it on with the first button on the start
  screen, the switch on the "Who's cooking?" screen, or in Settings. It turns on:
  * Audio Guide: reads dialogue, steps, "Why it matters" notes and results aloud.
    Choose "Game voice" or "My screen reader" (so the game does not talk over VoiceOver,
    TalkBack, NVDA or JAWS). Speech speed control and a Repeat button.
  * Sound cues: sizzle, bubbles, stirring, success and try-again chimes.
  * Extra spoken descriptions of the cafe and the kitchen scene.
  * Larger buttons.
  Each piece also has its own switch in Settings. Saved on this device.
- Always on for everyone: screen reader labels, headings and landmarks, announcements for
  every game event, focus moves to each new screen or step, full keyboard play
  (Tab, Enter, arrow keys, R to repeat the last announcement), works at 200% zoom.
- Timing and stirring work by sound and words, not just sight. In Relaxed mode nothing is
  timed: the pancake waits until you press Flip. "Check the pan" tells you how it is doing.
- Recipe cards have a "Cooking without sight: tips" section (talking thermometer for 160F/71C
  eggs and batter, tactile measuring, safe stove habits).
- Better color contrast throughout.

ALSO IN THIS APP
- NEW: "Who's cooking?" levels (change any time in Settings):
  * Little Chef (about ages 3 to 6): pretend play with big pictures, spoken prompts, one huge
    button per step, no failing, no timer, stickers. A grown-up icon marks stove and egg steps.
    Recipe card becomes "Cook With a Grown-Up" with Kid job / Grown-up job labels.
  * Junior Chef (about ages 7 to 11): simpler words, hints, grown-up badges on stove steps,
    relaxed mode only.
  * Chef (12 and up): the full game with Relaxed or Challenge mode.
- No accounts, ads, chat, purchases or data collection. Progress stays on the device.
- The Little Tide Café in the seaside town of Port Lumi.
- First customer: Mina Hoshino, a college student who needs an affordable breakfast.
- Cook Fluffy Pancakes step by step in the real order, with "Why it matters" for every step,
  real prep and cook times, and food safety checks.
- Relaxed mode (no timer, default) and Challenge mode (clock, tighter timing, speed bonus).
  Switch in Settings.
- Coins reward, Mina's reaction, friendship, and a Recipe Journal entry with her note and a tip.
- "Cook This for Real" recipe cards for all 6 opening dishes: pancakes, vegetable fried rice,
  tomato basil pasta, cheese omelet, salmon onigiri, chocolate chip cookies. Each has servings
  scaler, US and metric amounts, equipment, numbered steps, allergens, food safety,
  substitutions, a shopping list, print, and hands-free spoken steps.
- Decorate the café with coins (6 items that appear in the café scene).
- Large text, high contrast, reduce motion, keyboard and screen reader support.
- Progress saves on the device it is played on.

Recipes are standard home recipes. Always check food labels for allergens.
